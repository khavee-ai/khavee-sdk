<objective>
Add 4 research-backed animation layers to `VRMAvatar` that make chatStatus transitions feel psychologically natural:

1. **Nodding** — during `listening`, probabilistic procedural nods (3 types) fire every 2–4 s
2. **Thinking pose** — on `thinking` entry, head tilts sideways (Y axis), gaze shifts downward
3. **Gaze aversion** — during `thinking`, the lookAt target occasionally drifts off-centre then returns
4. **Volume-reactive movement** — during `speaking`, head micro-movement amplitude scales with `currentVolume`
5. **Keyword gesture** — when entering `speaking`, last assistant message is scanned for keywords; if a matching FBX animation key exists, it is played instead of the random pick

All bone deltas AFTER `mixer.update(delta)`, BEFORE `vrm.update(delta)`. All state in `useRef`. Zero new packages.
