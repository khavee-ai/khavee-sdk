<objective>
Build the shared module's two remaining internal pieces on top of 10-01's engine: (1) the migrated blink procedural-delta layer (D-01) as a ref-driven stepper that writes through the format adapter, and (2) the `AnimationStateEngine` — a `resolveBaseClip` pure function (chatStatus + manual `animate()` override -> target base-clip name) plus a `useAnimationController` hook that triggers 10-01's crossfade on target change and drives the per-frame ramp + blink from a single `update(delta)` call. Purpose: Prove the procedural delta layer works end-to-end with one real behavior (blink) and give both components a single, timer-free, adapter-driven controller to wire into their existing `useFrame`.
Output: `packages/react/src/animation/{blink.ts,AnimationStateEngine.ts,AnimationStateEngine.test.ts}`.
