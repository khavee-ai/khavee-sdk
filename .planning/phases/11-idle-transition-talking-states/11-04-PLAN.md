<objective>
Create the two speaking-state systems: `talkCycle.ts` (loop-completion-driven cycling across 2+ talk-clip variants with a ~2s minimum dwell, zero live-clock timers) and `audioAmplitude.ts` (a pure volume→amplitude-scale mapping active only during `speaking`).

Purpose: TALK-01 requires talk-clip variants to switch ONLY at natural loop boundaries with a minimum dwell — `resolveBaseClip`'s `.find()` always returns the first match and never cycles (RESEARCH Pitfall 4). TALK-02 requires live volume to scale procedural amplitude during `speaking` only, never affecting clip selection or timing. Keeping crossfade/blend state in the controller (single owner), `talkCycle` here is a decision function: it detects the boundary + dwell and NAMES the next variant; Plan 05's controller performs the actual `beginCrossfade`.
Output: `talkCycle.ts`, `audioAmplitude.ts` (both internal), plus unit tests.
</objective>
