<objective>
Close the four confirmed-failing Phase 11 behaviors from 11-06-SUMMARY.md by fixing three diagnosed, code-level animation-timing bugs (they are timing/effect-stability defects, not new design decisions):

1. TALK-01 talk-variant snap-back and TRANS-01 start-crossfade double-trigger — both caused by the crossfade-trigger `useEffect` re-firing on every render (an inline, non-memoized `getAction` closure is listed in its dependency array, and re-renders are frequent during speaking because `currentVolume` updates every volume tick). Fixed by dropping `getAction` from the effect deps, memoizing `getAction`/`getRoot` in both avatars, and adding a speaking-variant ownership guard so `talkCycle` is the sole owner of talk-variant selection during `speaking`.
2. TRANS-02 stop-settle snap — caused by `settleScale` being applied as an instant binary cut (`chatStatus === "stopped" ? 0.15 : 1`). Fixed by easing the settle scale toward its target over the same ~1.2s floor window (both directions) with `easeInOutCubic`.
3. IDLE-02 invisible expression drift — caused by two compounding bugs: (a) `DRIFT_CANDIDATES = ["neutral", "browInnerUp"]` are either absent or visually inert on the bundled VRoid VRM 0.x models, and (b) the runtime guard `if (current !== 0) continue` freezes drift after the first frame because it reads back drift's own prior write. Fixed by choosing present+visible candidates and replacing the bare non-zero guard with drift-ownership tracking.

Purpose: restore the four behaviors a human reported failing on a live build (11-06-SUMMARY Task 2) so the phase can pass re-verification.
Output: corrected `AnimationStateEngine.ts`, `expressionDrift.ts` (+ new test), and memoized accessors in both avatar components.
</objective>
