<objective>
Sixth gap-closure fix pass for Phase 11. Close the two open findings from 11-16's decisive human checkpoint (recorded verbatim in 11-16-SUMMARY.md):

1. **VRM leg sway (OPEN ISSUE 1)** — Human: "the sway/breath should not affect the legs cause its weird seeing legs also sway." Root cause is structural and already isolated by reading the source: `sway.ts`'s `stepSway` writes its rotation delta to the `hips` bone (`hips.quaternion.multiply(...)`). In a humanoid rig the `hips` bone is the skeleton root, and both upper-leg bones are its direct children — so any rotation applied to `hips` rotates the entire lower body, legs included. `breathing.ts` writes only `chest`+`spine` (an upper-body subtree that contains no leg bones), so it is NOT a leg-affecting write. The fix retargets sway away from `hips` and onto `spine`+`chest` (both already captured in `RestPoseAnchor`, so no new near-zero-weight accumulation surface is introduced).

2. **GLB sway too strong after animation change (OPEN ISSUE 2)** — Human: "the glb side sway/breath is fine until i changed its animation, the sway intensity is too much. may be turn that off when animating?" `proceduralScale` in `AnimationStateEngine.ts`'s `update()` is currently computed only from `amplitudeScale` (speaking volume) × `settleScale` (stopped settle ramp) — it is completely independent of which base clip is active. When the GLB user parks the avatar on a manually-selected non-idle clip via `animate()`, procedural breathing+sway keep applying their additive `.multiply()` deltas on top of a clip that may not fully drive spine/hips every frame, reading as excessive. The fix gates procedural body motion (breathing+sway) OFF on the GLB path whenever a manually-selected, non-idle clip is the active base — exactly the human's suggested "turn that off when animating," while leaving the default idle clip's approved procedural motion untouched.

Purpose: Unblock Phase 11 completion by resolving both open findings without regressing any of the five previously-fixed defects (11-09 first-load spin, 11-11 G2 idle→talk snap, 11-13 G4 talk-cycle jiggle, 11-13 G1/G3 pre-connect/connect drop, 11-15 G5 page-load Y-drop) or the 7 phase requirements.
Output: Modified sway target + a new GLB-scoped procedural gate, both unit-tested; full suite green; tsc clean. Decisive live confirmation is deferred to 11-18's human checkpoint (this phase's established diagnose-fix-then-re-verify pattern).
