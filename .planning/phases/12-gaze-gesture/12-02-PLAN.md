<objective>
Build `gaze.ts` — the continuous, camera-relative soft-gaze procedural system (GAZE-01/02) — as a new internal animation module following `breathing.ts`'s exact hook/step/scratch-quaternion shape, plus the fixed `thinking` aversion offset. This is the phase's one genuinely novel piece of math (target-tracking vs. fixed-axis sine), so it is scoped as its own plan with an early empirical head-bone-axis spike.

Purpose: GAZE-01 requires soft camera-relative gaze in `ready`/`listening`/`speaking`, brief aversion in `thinking`, and NO gaze treatment in `starting`/`stopped`. GAZE-02 requires identical bone-level behavior on VRM and GLB. RESEARCH flags `Object3D.lookAt()` as an absolute overwrite that violates PERF-01 — gaze must compute an absolute target in scratch, diff to a delta, clamp the delta angle, and apply additively via `multiply()`. The bundled rigs' head-bone local-forward convention is an Open Question that must be resolved empirically before the delta math is trusted.

Output: `gaze.ts` (new, not exported from index.ts), `gaze.test.ts`, and a committed headless verification script.
</objective>
