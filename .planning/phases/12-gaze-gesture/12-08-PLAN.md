<objective>
Close Gap 2 from 12-06-VERIFICATION.md: on the GLB avatar specifically ("only glb ... in idle animation the model spin weird"), the model spins/twists in an unexpected way during idle animation. This does NOT reproduce on VRM. Root cause is not yet diagnosed — this plan first investigates empirically, then implements the fix.

Leading hypothesis (to CONFIRM, not assume): `gaze.ts` computes its world target via `_scratchWorldTarget.setFromUnitVectors(HEAD_FORWARD_AXIS, direction)` where `HEAD_FORWARD_AXIS = (0,0,-1)` is treated as the head's forward in WORLD space. `VRMAvatar.tsx` mounts its group with `rotation = [0, Math.PI, 0]` (180 degrees about Y), so the VRM head's world forward points toward a +Z camera and the gaze delta is a few degrees. `GLBAvatar.tsx` (and the `/glb-avatar-test` page, which mounts `<GLBAvatar src="/models/happy.glb" />` with the default `rotation = [0,0,0]`) leaves the head facing world -Z — roughly 180 degrees away from the camera. The clamp caps the per-frame delta at MAX_GAZE_ANGLE_RAD, but the target is ~180 degrees away, so gaze applies a persistent maximum off-axis twist every frame that fights the idle clip and reads as a spin. The correct behavior is a small offset toward the camera regardless of how the consuming app orients the avatar group (GAZE-02 = bone-level symmetry).

Purpose: GAZE-02 FAILs partly on this GLB-only regression. It is the last blocker (with Gap 1) before Phase 12 can close.
Output: A confirmed root-cause note (in the SUMMARY) plus a group-rotation-agnostic fix in `gaze.ts` and a regression test.
</objective>
